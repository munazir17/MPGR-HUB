# MPGR Tokenomics

## Overview

**Token Name:** MoneyPaiger

**Symbol:** MPGR

**Network:** Base

**Maximum Supply:** 1,000,000,000 MPGR

The MPGR ecosystem follows a community-first token economy designed for long-term sustainability, ecosystem growth, and real on-chain utility.

There are:

- No inflation
- No future minting
- No private sale
- No VC allocation
- No locked team allocation

The maximum supply is permanently fixed at **1 Billion MPGR**.

# Treasury Flexibility

The allocations above represent the initial treasury plan.

Unused allocations are **not permanently locked** to any category.

Future governance may reallocate undistributed treasury tokens between ecosystem programs based on community needs while keeping the total treasury allocation fixed at **100,000,000 MPGR**.

---

# Emission Model

MPGR follows a dynamic emission model.

Rewards are distributed based on:

- User activity
- Treasury availability
- Staking participation
- Community growth
- Seasonal campaigns
- Governance decisions

No category emits tokens indefinitely.

Emission rates may be adjusted over time to preserve long-term sustainability.

---
