# MPGR Governance

## Overview

MPGR Governance is the future decision-making framework of the MPGR ecosystem.

Its purpose is to give the community a transparent and decentralized method of influencing ecosystem growth while ensuring long-term sustainability.

Governance will be introduced in phases as the ecosystem matures.

---

# Vision

The long-term vision is to transition key ecosystem decisions from the development team to the MPGR community.

Governance will empower active community members to help shape the future of MPGR HUB.

---

# Objectives

Community participation

Treasury transparency

Sustainable ecosystem growth

Fair reward distribution

Long-term decentralization

---

# Governance Scope

Future governance may include decisions related to:

Treasury allocation

Reward budgets

Staking parameters

Campaign funding

Partnership approvals

Community initiatives

New ecosystem features

AI ecosystem expansion

Premium feature development

Protocol upgrades

---

# Treasury Governance

Community governance may vote on:

Reward allocation adjustments

Season budgets

Special campaigns

Marketing incentives

Ecosystem grants

Emergency treasury usage

Unused treasury reallocations

The Community Treasury will always remain capped at its allocated supply.

No governance proposal may create new MPGR tokens.

---

# Proposal System

Future governance will support:

Proposal creation

Proposal discussion

Community review

Voting period

Execution after approval

Transparent proposal history

---

# Voting

Voting power may be based on one or more of the following:

MPGR holdings

Staked MPGR

Premium membership

Community reputation

Long-term participation

Future governance models may combine multiple voting signals.

# Security

Governance actions affecting treasury or protocol parameters may require:

Voting thresholds

Minimum participation

Execution delays

Multi-signature approval where appropriate

Emergency safeguards

---

# Transparency

All governance activities should remain publicly visible.

This includes:

Active proposals

Voting results

Treasury decisions

Historical records

Executed proposals

---

# Future DAO

As MPGR grows, governance may evolve into a fully decentralized autonomous organization (DAO).

The DAO may oversee:

Treasury management

Protocol upgrades

Reward strategy

Ecosystem expansion

Partnership approvals

Community funding

---

# Long-Term Vision

Governance is intended to make MPGR a community-driven ecosystem where users actively participate in shaping the future of the platform.

The ultimate goal is to create a transparent, decentralized, and sustainable governance model that aligns the interests of token holders, builders, and the broader Base ecosystem.
